Visual inequality checker

Enter constants and see f(n) against c·g(n). The checker tests every integer n from n₀ to n₀ + 400, then sparser points up to 10¹⁰⁰, and uses the limit to judge "all large n". Finite numerical testing is not a mathematical proof - you still have to show the inequality algebraically.
